## Interpretation

**Result (frozen, PREREG_aging_hlca §6).** **isotropic noise increase;
no aging claim beyond stiffness — no majority stiffness change.**
Isotropy 0/13, anticipation 1/13, stiffness loss 1/13. The new
qualifier did its job: the header is not read as "operators widened".

**What it means.**
1. **No direction-specific aging in any human lung cell type.** 0 of 13
   primary; 1 of 13 in the within-study and secondary-space analyses
   (different types each time: Multiciliated, Macrophages), i.e. chance.
   The synthetic checks had full power for one direction changing ~2×
   over the age range, so effects that large are unlikely in any type;
   smaller ones are not excluded.
2. **The two single hits are fragile.**
   * *T cell lineage softening* (slope −0.0056 per year in log stiffness,
     ≈ 28 % softer over 60 years; p = 0.005). Holds in never-smokers
     (p = 0.006), but not within studies (same slope, p = 0.19), not in
     parenchyma (0.08), and vanishes in the all-ages HLCA space (slope
     0.000, p = 0.73). Most of its evidence comes from between-study age
     differences. Not significant after correcting for 13 types
     (Bonferroni 0.065). A lead, not a finding.
   * *EC capillary anticipation* (p = 0.047; within-study 0.040; 0.06–0.07
     elsewhere): consistently borderline, 24 donors with only 5 aged ≥ 60;
     ≈ 49 % chance of ≥ 1 such pass in 13 types under the null.
   * A weak, consistent hint: *Monocytes* soften in three analyses
     (primary p = 0.077, never-smokers 0.012, secondary 0.039).
3. **Mouse and human agree.** Both arms find no robust aging change in
   operator shape; the matched types (fibroblasts, NK cells) are null in
   both species.

**Caveats.** 17 donors ≥ 60, clustered in two studies; study and age are
partly confounded (the within-study analysis is the check, and it
agrees on the null). Older donors' cells fit the young reference less
well (H03), but the all-ages space gives the same null. One scVI seed.

**Manus's observations.** All hold up.
