## Interpretation (exploratory; H04's frozen outcome is unchanged)

1. **Uniform softening — well powered.** Every type reaches 80 % power;
   median MDE ×1.5 over ages 10–76 (range ×1.1–×3; fibroblasts ×3,
   EC capillary ×2). The H04 null therefore **rules out uniform
   stiffness loss of ≥ 1.5-fold in most human lung types**.
2. **Drift along the young soft axis — moderately powered.** Every type
   reaches 80 %; median MDE 2 σ (range 0.5–4 σ), σ = the young
   within-type spread along that axis. Drifts smaller than ~2 σ over
   the age range are not excluded.
3. **Single-axis softening — essentially no power.** Only AT2 reaches
   80 % (at ×3); 12 of 13 types do not by ×8, and power is
   non-monotonic in several (NK 0.66 at ×2, 0.40 at ×8). Cause (Claude,
   from the reference spectra): the isotropy statistic uses
   `log |λ|` ratios, and the soft direction's eigenvalue is often near
   zero (Macrophages −0.011) or positive (Basal +2.72). Near zero the log
   ratio is noise-dominated; on a positive (unstable) direction,
   "softening" moves the eigenvalue toward zero. AT2's soft eigenvalue is
   clearly negative (−0.58) and its test works. **So H04's "no
   direction-specific change" is uninformative for single-axis loss in
   12 of 13 types**; it must not be read as evidence of absence.
4. **The no-injection row is the real data, not a pure null.** Baseline
   "pass rates" of 0.96 (T cell stiffness), 0.34 (EC capillary
   anticipation), 0.24 (EC venous stiffness) and 0.22 (Monocytes
   stiffness) are the borderline real signals from H04, not false-
   positive rates. The MDEs of those types are measured on top of an
   existing effect and are optimistic.

**Methodological consequence.** The same statistic is the mouse §4b
test. The three mouse basins have clearly negative soft eigenvalues
(lead −6.9, −13.0, −1.5), so the mouse basin results are less affected;
the mouse non-basin types are affected in the same way.
