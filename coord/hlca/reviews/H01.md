## Interpretation — what the inventory means for the design (input to H02)
1. **Old donors are few and clustered.** 17 donors ≥ 60 across 8
   studies; 10 of them come from two studies (Banovich_Kropski_2020 5,
   Seibold_2020_10Xv3 5). Several studies are young-only (Barbry_Leroy
   10 donors, all < 45).
2. **Study, assay and site are confounded with age.** Seibold v3 is
   old-heavy and is most of the 10x 3' v3 data (old 5/17 on v3 vs young
   3/48). Airway-region sampling (score 0.36) is 5/17 of old vs 6/48 of
   young. Parenchyma (0.97) is the majority site for both.
3. **Smoking and sex are roughly balanced** (never-smokers: old 11/17,
   young 23/48; female: old 7/17, young 20/48).
4. **Only one study supports a real within-study contrast:**
   Banovich_Kropski_2020 (17 young, 5 old). The other five have 1–2
   old donors each.
5. **Lesson from the mouse arm (X05):** the cell-level split-half null
   ignores donor-to-donor variation. With 17 old donors the human arm
   can and should make a **donor-level** test primary.
