# hlca/H05 — Figures, mouse–human comparison, write-up

## Goal

Render the human result from stored outputs only, build the
pre-registered mouse–human comparison (PREREG_aging_hlca §8,
**descriptive, no statistic**), and complete `RESULT.md` and an executed
notebook. Nothing is refitted or recomputed beyond reading stored
tables and drift files.

## Inputs (check sha256 against the H03/H04 reports)

`benchmarks/aging_hlca/{tests_primary,tests_sensitivity,donor_operators,eligibility}.csv`,
`benchmarks/aging_hlca/qc/*`, `benchmarks/aging_hlca/data/h03_primary_drift.h5ad`;
mouse: `benchmarks/aging/compare_pooled.csv` (A03),
`benchmarks/aging/x04/summary.csv` (X04).

## Steps (`scripts/hlca_figures.py`, `scripts/make_hlca_notebook.py`)

Figures under `benchmarks/figures/aging_hlca/`, PDF + PNG at 200 dpi,
footer `PREREG_aging_hlca · hlca/H04 frozen tests · scArches young-only
reference · d_latent=15 · units relative`:

1. `tests_overview` — per type (13 rows): isotropy `S` vs its `q95`,
   anticipation statistic vs `q95`, stiffness slope with the permutation
   95 % band; passes marked.
2. `sensitivity_heatmap` — types × {primary, a–f}; each cell shows the
   iso / ant / stiffness calls; ineligible = grey.
3. `stiffness_vs_age` — 13 panels: per-donor `log median |λ|` against
   age, coloured by `study_or_dataset`, OLS line; T cell lineage and
   Monocytes flagged in the panel titles as "lead (H04 review)".
4. `ec_capillary_anticipation` — donor centroid projected on the
   reference soft axis against age, coloured by study.
5. `mouse_human` — the §8 pairs (mouse lung fibroblast ↔ Fibroblasts;
   mouse NK ↔ Innate lymphoid cell NK): mouse old/young stiffness ratio
   (A03 value and the X04 shared-seed range) next to human
   `exp(stiff_slope)` from analysis `f_two_group` (≥ 60 vs < 45), plus
   each side's isotropy and anticipation calls as text. Footer:
   "descriptive comparison; no statistic (PREREG_aging_hlca §8)".
6. QC: `scvi_history` (reference and query ELBO curves), `query_vs_ref_elbo`
   (final validation ELBO, the two bars), `symmetry` (primary and
   secondary `sym_max`), `eligibility_counts` (per analysis),
   `donor_composition` (donors by age bin × study).

Then:

7. `benchmarks/aging_hlca/mouse_human.csv` — one row per §8 pair, the
   numbers shown in figure 5.
8. `benchmarks/figures/aging_hlca/MANIFEST.csv`
   (`path, figure, source_files`).
9. `notebooks/aging_hlca.ipynb`, built by `make_hlca_notebook.py` and
   executed in place, reading committed CSVs (and the drift h5ad only
   where a figure needs it, through a `HLCA_DATA` path variable at the
   top). A markdown cell above each figure says what it shows and which
   rule produced it.
10. `RESULT.md`: add §8 Mouse–human comparison (the table), §9 Figures
    (every path), §10 Limits — copy verbatim the bullets under
    "Limits to copy verbatim" below, adding nothing interpretive.

## Limits to copy verbatim into RESULT.md §10

* The frozen outcome reports no direction-specific (0/13) and no
  majority anticipatory (1/13) change with age in human lung operators,
  and no majority change in stiffness (1/13 softening).
* The single stiffness hit (T cell lineage) does not hold within
  studies (p = 0.19), in parenchyma (p = 0.08) or in the all-ages HLCA
  space (p = 0.73), and is not significant after correction for 13
  types. The single anticipation hit (EC capillary, p = 0.047) is
  borderline in every analysis.
* 17 donors are aged ≥ 60 and cluster in two studies; age is partly
  confounded with study, assay and sampling site. Cells of older donors
  fit the young-only reference less well (validation ELBO ≈ 655 vs
  ≈ 550). One scVI seed. Effects smaller than about a two-fold change
  of one direction over the age range are not excluded.
* The mouse–human comparison covers two matched cell types and is
  descriptive.
