# hlca/HX01 — T cell and monocyte softening hints (EXPLORATORY)

**Exploratory.** Nothing here changes H04 or RESULT.md §1–§10. First
line of the report and `RESULT_HX01.md`: "EXPLORATORY". No model is
fitted.

## Question

H04's T cell lineage stiffness slope (−0.0056/yr, p = 0.005) and the
weak Monocytes hint (p = 0.077; never-smokers 0.012) did not hold within
studies. Are they (a) a shift in subtype composition with age, or (b)
driven by particular studies?

## Inputs (check sha256 against H01/H03)

`benchmarks/aging_hlca/data/h03_primary_drift.h5ad` (the drift subsample:
latent, per-cell `J`, donor metadata);
`hlca_core_full_metadata.h5ad` for `ann_level_4` by cell ID;
`benchmarks/aging_hlca/donor_operators.csv` (H04).

## Steps (`scripts/hlca_hx01.py`; seed 0)

1. Join `ann_level_4` onto the subsample cells of T cell lineage and
   Monocytes by cell ID; report unmatched cells.
2. **Composition:** per donor, fraction CD8 among CD4 + CD8 T cells, and
   fraction non-classical among monocytes (from the **full** metadata,
   all cells of the donor). Spearman with age; and with the donor's H04
   `log_median_abs_lambda` for that type.
3. **Subtype stiffness:** `stiffness_trend` per subtype (CD4 T cells,
   CD8 T cells, Classical monocytes, Non-classical monocytes) on the
   subsample, with `n_common = 25` and a 25-cell donor floor (the
   subsample holds ≤ 60 cells per donor × `ann_level_3`), `n_draws=100`,
   `n_perm=1000`; report donors per subtype.
4. **Study dependence:** for T cell lineage and Monocytes, recompute the
   H04 stiffness slope and p (`n_perm=1000`) **leaving out one study at
   a time** (from H04's donor operators, no recomputation of operators);
   and the slope from an OLS of `log_median_abs_lambda` on age with study
   fixed effects (descriptive).
5. **Reading rule (fixed before running):**
   * a hint is **composition-explained** if the subtype slopes (step 3)
     are both |slope| < half the H04 slope **and** the composition
     fraction correlates with age at p ≤ 0.05;
   * a hint is **study-driven** if leaving out any single study moves p
     above 0.05 **or** the study-fixed-effects slope is < half the H04
     slope in magnitude;
   * otherwise **unexplained**.
6. Figures (`benchmarks/figures/aging_hlca/hx01/`; footer
   "EXPLORATORY · hlca/HX01"): composition vs age; subtype stiffness vs
   age by study; leave-one-study-out slopes with CIs.
7. `benchmarks/aging_hlca/hx01/RESULT_HX01.md`: what ran, rule verbatim,
   tables, verdicts. No other interpretation.
