# hlca/H03 — HLCA preprocessing, shared spaces, energy drift

## Summary
H03 is **done**.  It retained 106 numeric-age HLCA donors (581,517 cells),
selected 2,000 frozen HVGs with exact full-data Scanpy equivalence, and built a
56,904-cell deterministic drift sample.  The CPU-only primary scVI reference
completed 400 epochs; the frozen scArches query early-stopped at 88 epochs with
an exact frozen latent-encoder/BatchNorm audit of 0.0.  Exact ESM energy drift
completed in both the 15-D primary and 30-D published secondary spaces, and
both passed the `sym_max ≤ 1e-4` gate.  Eligibility retains 13 primary, 12
parenchyma-only, 6 never-smoker, and 2 Banovich-only types.  No §5 statistic,
outcome header, or sensitivity test was computed in H03.
