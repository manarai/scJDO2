# perturb/P02 — Does control-only gene coupling predict held-out knockdown responses?

Issue only after P01 is accepted with F1–F3 passing. Targets, gene set B,
control cells and fit settings are P01's, unchanged. Cloud sandbox rules
as in `coord/PROJECT.md`.

## Design (fixed at issue)

* **Targets:** the covered qualifying targets in P01's `coverage.csv`
  (frozen list; nothing added or dropped after this point).
* **Fits:** P01's control-only operator pipeline with fit seeds 0, 1, 2
  (same 2,000 control cells; drift and gene field refit per seed).
* **Operator prediction:** M_a(t*) = Pᵀ(e^{B_a t*} − I)P per archetype a
  (block, projector, t* from `operator_tables`); M̄ = Σ_a w̄_a M_a, where
  w̄_a = mean membership of archetype a over the 2,000 control cells.
  Prediction Δ̂_j = M̄[:, j] · δ_j, δ_j = observed Δ_j of gene j itself.
* **Score:** per target, Pearson r between Δ̂_j and Δ_j over B without j.
* **Baselines** (same score):
  1. co-expression: Δ̂_j = ρ[:, j] · δ_j, ρ = Pearson correlation of L
     among the 2,000 controls;
  2. covariance: Δ̂_j = Σ[:, j] / Σ[j, j] · δ_j (regression of each gene on
     gene j in controls);
  3. mean shift: mean Δ over the training-fold targets;
  4. linear: ridge (α = 1, multi-output) from gene j's loadings on the
     top 10 control PCs to Δ_j, trained on training-fold targets.
  Baselines 3–4 use 5 folds over targets (`KFold(5, shuffle=True,
  random_state=0)`); every target is scored once, out of fold.
* **Null:** for each target, replace column j of M̄ by the column of a
  random other covered target (200 draws, `default_rng(0)`); score.

## Gates (fixed 2026-11-08, confirmed by the user before any P01 result)

* **G1 beats co-expression:** median over targets of (r_op − r_coexp) > 0
  with one-sided paired Wilcoxon p < 0.01, and the same for covariance, in
  ≥ 2 of 3 fit seeds.
* **G2 beats trained baselines:** median r_op > median r of mean shift and
  of the linear baseline, in ≥ 2 of 3 fit seeds.
* **G3 specific to the target:** median r_op ≥ 0.10 and above the null's
  95th percentile, in ≥ 2 of 3 fit seeds.

Readings: G1 ∧ G2 ∧ G3 — control-only coupling carries causal information
on K562; P03 (TF Atlas) may be written. G1 fails — coupling is no better
than co-expression at predicting interventions; "coupling is not
regulation" becomes a measured result, and P03 is not run. Other
combinations are reported as they are, with no claim.
