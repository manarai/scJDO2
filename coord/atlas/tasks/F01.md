## Why

Before claiming that operator motifs recur across biology, the archetype shapes must be stable across
biological replicates **within one dataset**. This has never been tested. LARRY (Weinreb et al. 2020,
in vitro) has the needed design: day 2 is a single well (0), and days 4 and 6 were sampled from two
independent wells (1 and 2) re-plated from it. Two flow fits that share the day-2 cells but use
different wells for days 4 and 6 give two archetype labellings of **the same day-2 cells**, so they can
be compared cell by cell. Within-replicate refits (drift seeds) set how much agreement the fitting
noise alone allows.

## Steps (scripts `scripts/atlas_f01_*.py`; outputs under `benchmarks/atlas/f01/`)

1. **Inventory (no fitting).** Confirm `.X` holds raw counts; report the table
   `Time point × Well × Starting population` and `Cell type annotation` counts per day × well →
   `inventory.md`. Expected: day 2 only well 0; days 4 and 6 wells 1 and 2.
2. **Matched subsampling (numpy seed 0).** Day 2: all cells. For each of day 4 and day 6 and each
   starting population (LSK, LK), take `m = min(count in well 1, count in well 2)` cells at random from
   **each** well, so both wells have the same size and the same LSK/LK mix per day (balanced transport
   would otherwise move a composition difference between wells as state change). Record the cell
   barcodes → `cells.csv` (`obs_name, day, well, replicate`) with replicate A = well 1, B = well 2
   (day-2 cells belong to both).
3. **One shared frame.** On the union of all cells from step 2: `normalize_total(1e4)`, `log1p`,
   2,000 HVGs (scanpy `seurat`), PCA 15 components (`random_state=0`) → `obsm["X_pca"]`. Both replicates
   use these coordinates (this tests the estimate, not the frame).
4. **Fits.** For replicate r ∈ {A, B} and drift seed s ∈ {0, 1, 2}: the cells of r (day 2 + its well
   on days 4, 6), `scjdo2.flow(a, time_key="day", n_comps=15)` with all other settings at their
   defaults (eps 0.05, MLP (96,96,96), 1,500 epochs, `keep_plan=100`), `seed=s`. 6 fits. Save per fit:
   `J` (float32), `obs`, `fit_meta.json` (cells, seed, wall time, peak RSS, OT diagnostics).
5. **K, fixed once.** `scjdo2.flow_archetypes(fit A, s=0, n_archetypes="auto",
   k_candidates=(2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12))` → `K*`. If `K*` = 12 (boundary), use 12 and report it.
   Then refit archetypes on **every** fit with `n_archetypes=K*`, all other arguments default
   (Frobenius-normalised, `max_fit_cells=4000`, `seed=0`).
6. **Agreement on the day-2 cells** (same cells in all 6 fits):
   * **ARI** of `obs["flow_archetype"]` between fits, restricted to day-2 cells;
   * **shape cosine:** match the K archetype patterns of two fits by the Hungarian algorithm on
     |cosine| of the flattened patterns; report each matched cosine (signed) and the minimum;
   * **per-cell λ₁ Spearman** on day-2 cells (report only);
   * **transitions (report only):** `scjdo2.flow_transitions(fit, n_perm=1000)` for the 2 → 4 pair,
     with labels mapped to replicate A s = 0 via the matching; report the matched `prob` matrices and the
     mean absolute difference between A and B.
   Pairs: **replicate** (A_s, B_s) for s = 0, 1, 2; **refit** (A_s, A_s′) and (B_s, B_s′) for the three
   seed pairs each. → `agreement.csv` (`comparison, pair, metric, value`), `matched_cosines.csv`,
   `transitions_2to4.csv`.
7. `RESULT_F01.md`: what ran, the gate verbatim, the gate table, the verdict. No interpretation beyond
   the gate. Figures: `figures/archetypes_A0.png`, `figures/archetypes_B0.png`
   (`plot.figure_flow_archetypes`) and `figures/transitions_A0.png`, `figures/transitions_B0.png`.

## Gate (fixed before any fit)

| id | condition | pass |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | finite J | ≥ 99 % of cells in every fit |
| F1 | label agreement across replicates | median over s of ARI(A_s, B_s) on day-2 cells ≥ **0.70** |
| F2 | shape agreement across replicates | for s = 0, the minimum matched shape cosine of (A_0, B_0) ≥ **0.80** |
| F3 | replicate noise not above refit noise | median replicate ARI ≥ **0.8 ×** median refit ARI (all 6 refit pairs) |

Verdict: **stable** if G0, F1, F2 and F3 pass; otherwise **not stable**, with the failing ids. Reading
for the workstream: cross-dataset motif work (premise 3) is built only on a stable result. A fail is a
result, not a reason to change settings (no other K, normalisation, epochs, subsampling or space).
