## Question

F01 found the flow operator itself reproducible (per-cell λ₁ ρ ≈ 0.97 across wells and seeds), but
not its 12-archetype split. The automatic K sat at the boundary (12), and held-out error kept falling.
Does a **smaller** K give archetypes that reproduce across replicate wells, as well as across drift
seeds? Two possible answers:
* yes, for some K ≥ 3: motifs exist at a coarse level, and that K is the unit for later atlas work;
* only at K = 2, or at no K: the archetype layer shouldn't be the unit of the atlas.

## Steps (`scripts/atlas_f02_ksweep.py`; outputs under `benchmarks/atlas/f02/`)

1. **Rebuild the six fit objects** from the files above: the rows of `shared_frame.h5ad` named in
   `obs_<tag>.csv`, in that order, with `obs` from that csv and J from `J_<tag>.npz` placed where
   `scjdo2.flow_archetypes` reads it (`uns["scjdo2"]["J"]`, with whatever metadata it checks,
   e.g. `meta.operator = "flow"`). Don't refit the flow.
2. **Reproduction check (must pass before step 3).** With `n_archetypes=12`,
   `normalize="frobenius"`, `max_fit_cells=4000`, `seed=0` (F01's arguments), recompute ARI(A0, B0)
   on day-2 cells and the A0–B0 matched cosines. They must equal F01's `agreement.csv` and
   `matched_cosines.csv` within 1e-6. If not, stop and ask: report the differences, and don't continue
   with a non-reproducing pipeline.
3. **K sweep.** For each K ∈ {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8} and each of the six fits: `scjdo2.flow_archetypes(fit,
   n_archetypes=K)` with F01's other arguments. Per K, on **day-2 cells** (shared by all six fits),
   using F01's code for ARI and for Hungarian matching on |cosine| (`cosine_match` in
   `scripts/atlas_f01_fit.py`):
   * **replicate ARI** (A_s, B_s), s = 0, 1, 2; **refit ARI** for the six seed pairs (A_s, A_s′) and
     (B_s, B_s′);
   * **matched cosines** (signed) for every replicate and refit pair; minimum and median per pair;
   * **per-archetype stability:** for each archetype of A0, in each of the other five fits, its
     matched cosine and the Jaccard index of its day-2 cell set with the matched archetype's.
   → `agreement.csv` (`K, comparison, pair, metric, value`), `matched_cosines.csv`
   (`K, pair, A_index, B_index, signed_cosine`), `per_archetype.csv` (`K, archetype, other_fit,
   cosine, jaccard, size_A0`).
4. **Reading rule (fixed before step 3).** At each K, apply F01's gate unchanged: F1 median
   replicate ARI ≥ 0.70; F2 minimum matched cosine (A0, B0) ≥ 0.80; F3 median replicate ARI ≥ 0.8 ×
   median refit ARI. A K is **reproducible** if F1, F2 and F3 all pass. An archetype is **stable** at
   K if, in all five other fits, its matched cosine is ≥ 0.80 and its Jaccard is ≥ 0.50. Report:
   `largest_reproducible_K` (`none` if none), and per K the pass/fail of F1–F3 and the number of stable
   archetypes. Reading: **coarse motifs** if `largest_reproducible_K` ≥ 3; **K = 2 only** if only 2;
   **no reproducible K** otherwise.
5. `RESULT_F02.md`: the first line above, what ran, the rule verbatim, the table per K, the reading.
   No other interpretation. Figure: `figures/ksweep.png`, with median replicate ARI and median refit ARI
   vs K, the 0.70 line, and the min matched cosine (A0, B0) vs K with the 0.80 line, both panels
   with F01's K = 12 point marked.
