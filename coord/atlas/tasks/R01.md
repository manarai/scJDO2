## Goal

Before three hematopoiesis datasets are placed in one descriptor space
(R02), check within each dataset that the per-cell operator descriptors
(1) don't change much with the fit seed, (2) don't change much with
training length, and (3) agree between disjoint halves of the cells,
including halves split by sequencing batch. All three datasets are
fitted with **one identical pipeline**, so differences in R02 can't come
from method differences. Gene-level (driver) motifs are out of scope here.

## Steps (scripts `scripts/atlas_r01_*.py`; outputs under `benchmarks/atlas/r01/`)

1. **Inventory (no fitting).** Per dataset, report: what `.X` holds
   (raw counts vs log-normalised; check value ranges), which genes are
   present, the cell counts per lineage label, and for `dynamo` the
   cross-table `batch × time × cell_type`. Write
   `benchmarks/atlas/r01/inventory.md`. If any `.X` state is ambiguous,
   stop and ask.
2. **Identical preprocessing.** From the state found in step 1, bring each
   dataset to log1p(counts per 10⁴), 2,000 HVGs (scanpy `seurat` flavour,
   computed within the dataset), PCA with **10 components** (`random_state=0`).
   Then divide the 10 PC scores by **one scalar**, the square root of the
   mean per-component variance, so all datasets have mean component
   variance 1 (relative variances kept). → `obsm["X_ref"]`.
   Pseudotime: scanpy diffusion pseudotime (15 neighbours on `X_ref`,
   `diffmap` 10 components, root = the cell nearest the centroid of the
   root group in the table) → `obs["ref_pt"]`. The same code for all three.
3. **Identical drift fit.** `scjdo2.pipeline.fit_latent_field(a,
   use_rep="X_ref", time_key="ref_pt", jacobian_method="autodiff",
   drift_config=DriftConfig(hidden=96, depth=3, n_epochs=E, seed=s,
   use_spectral_norm=False, batch_size=256))`, with all other settings at
   their defaults (the configuration of `scripts/run_setty_v2.py`). Snapshot
   DSM for all three, including `dynamo`: don't use `MeasuredDrift` or
   labelling velocity here. Call `torch.set_num_threads(...)` per SANDBOX.md.
   Fits (20 in all):
   * **seeds:** each dataset × `s ∈ {0, 1, 2}` at `E = 1500` (9 fits);
   * **length:** each dataset, `s = 0`, `E = 15000` (3 fits);
   * **random halves:** each dataset, cells split 50/50 at random
     (`numpy` seed 0, stratified by lineage label), each half refitted
     from step 2 (its own HVGs, PCA, scaling, pseudotime), `s = 0`,
     `E = 1500` (6 fits);
   * **batch halves (dynamo only):** the 3 batches split into two groups
     with cell counts as equal as possible (report the grouping and its
     `time × cell_type` table), each group refitted from step 2, `s = 0`,
     `E = 1500` (2 fits).
   Save per fit: the latent J tensor, `X_ref`, `ref_pt`, labels, and a
   `fit_meta.json` (data sha256, E, s, cells, wall time, final loss).
4. **Descriptors.** Per cell, from the latent J (10 × 10):
   `x = [Re λ₁ … Re λ₁₀ (sorted descending) / σ, Henrici, stiffness / σ]`,
   where stiffness = −mean of the negative real parts (as in
   `motifs.motif_coordinates`), and σ = the median over all cells and all
   10 eigenvalues of `|Re λ|` **in that fit**. Divide by σ but don't
   subtract a mean: the scale is arbitrary (no clock), the location isn't.
   Also store `motifs.motif_coordinates(a)` (unchanged library function)
   for the partitions in step 5.
5. **Agreement metrics.** For each comparison pair (seed pairs 0–1, 0–2,
   1–2; length 1,500 vs 15,000 at s = 0; half A vs half B):
   * **type-level ρ:** for lineage labels with ≥ 30 cells in both fits,
     the per-type mean of each of the 12 descriptor columns; Spearman
     across types **per column**, then the median over the 12 columns.
     (Don't correlate the flattened types × columns matrix: the sorted
     eigenvalue columns would make that trivially high.)
   * **per-cell ρ** (same cells only, i.e. seed and length pairs): Spearman
     across cells per column, median over columns; also report Re λ₁ alone.
   * **partition ARI** (same cells only): `K` chosen once per dataset by the
     `motifs.fit_motifs(k="auto")` rule on the s = 0, E = 1,500 fit
     (`replicate_key="batch"` for dynamo, none otherwise); then KMeans
     (`n_init=5`, `random_state=0`) with that `K` on each fit's
     `motif_coordinates`; adjusted Rand index between the two labellings.
   * **holder fraction per type** (cells with all Re λ < 0) in each fit,
     to make visible the training-length swings seen in the aging runs.
   → `benchmarks/atlas/r01/agreement.csv` (`dataset, comparison, pair,
   metric, value`) and `holder_fraction.csv`.
6. `benchmarks/atlas/r01/RESULT_R01.md`: what ran, the gate verbatim, the
   gate table, per-dataset verdicts. No interpretation beyond the gate.

## Gate (fixed before any fit; evaluated per dataset)

| id | condition | pass |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | finite J and descriptors | ≥ 99 % of cells in every fit |
| S1 | seed stability, E = 1,500 | median over the 3 seed pairs of type-level ρ ≥ 0.70 **and** of partition ARI ≥ 0.50 |
| S2 | training length, 1,500 vs 15,000 | type-level ρ ≥ 0.70 **and** partition ARI ≥ 0.50 |
| H1 | random halves | type-level ρ ≥ 0.70 |
| B1 | batch halves (dynamo) | type-level ρ ≥ 0.70 **and** ≥ (random-half type-level ρ − 0.15) |

Dataset verdict: **usable** if G0, S1, S2 and H1 pass (and B1 for
dynamo); otherwise **not usable**, with the failing ids. Workstream
reading: R02 is built only on usable datasets, and needs at least two.
Report the numbers whatever the verdict. A fail is a result, not a
reason to change settings.
