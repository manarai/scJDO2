## Interpretation

**Result.** G0 pass; F1 fail (0.514 vs 0.70); F2 fail (−0.44 vs 0.80); F3 pass at the threshold (0.801 vs 0.80). Verdict, verbatim from the rule: **not stable**, failing F1, F2. Per WORKSTREAM reading, premise-3 (cross-dataset motif) work is not built on this result.

**Meaning (Claude's reading, beyond the gate).**
- The *operator* is reproducible across replicate wells: per-cell λ₁ Spearman 0.97 between replicates, the same as between drift seeds (0.96–0.98).
- The *archetype decomposition* of it is not, and the main source is the decomposition, not the biology: refits of the same well with a different drift seed agree only at ARI 0.59–0.65 and their worst matched shape is also poor (minimum matched cosine over the six refit pairs: −0.42 to 0.53; median of minima 0.41). Replicate wells are only slightly worse (F3 ratio 0.80). A between-well biological difference cannot be separated from fitting noise at K = 12.
- K* = 12 is the largest candidate, and held-out reconstruction error falls monotonically (12.2 → 6.3). There is no K where the error curve says "enough archetypes": J_flow shapes on LARRY look like a continuum, not a small set of discrete motifs. With 12 forced clusters on a continuum, label boundaries move with small changes in the fit.
- The large, coherent archetypes do recur: matched cosines include 0.94, 0.87, 0.82 in A0/B0 and up to 0.93 in A2/B2; A1 (the largest, ~12–17k cells, self-persistent 0.99 in the transitions) is stable. The failures are the small archetypes.

**Caveats.** One dataset, in vitro, 3 seeds per replicate; days 4/6 differ between replicates only in well, so "replicate" here is a well, not an independent experiment. Auto-K was frozen to one rule (held-out Frobenius); the failure is partly a property of that rule choosing the boundary. The mini-batch OT reported Sinkhorn non-convergence warnings in several batches; λ₁ agreement suggests this did not destabilise the operator.

**Executor's observations.** All four hold (K at boundary, 100% finite, starting-population names, Sinkhorn warnings). The suggestion (which archetypes drive the gap) is adopted in adapted form below.
