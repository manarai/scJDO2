## Observations
- Lung fibroblast has only 21 distinct 2-young/5-old labelings; its smallest attainable p is 1/21 ≈ 0.04762. Seed 0 fails (p=0.1838) while seeds 1 and 2 each pass (p=0.04595), so the fixed rule does not pass it.

- All three single-axis grids pass first at F=1.25.
- Uniform ×2 passes for lung NK only. Its slope/p exactly reproduce the seed-0 real row, as required by the statistic's scale invariance, so it is not a uniform-injection-specific effect.
