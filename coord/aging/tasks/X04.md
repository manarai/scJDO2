# aging/X04 — Mapping-shrinkage check (EXPLORATORY)

**Exploratory.** Nothing here changes the frozen A03 outcome or
RESULT.md. Results go in their own folder and are labelled exploratory
everywhere. State "EXPLORATORY" in the first line of your report.

## Question

A03 found no stiffness loss with age (old/young median |λ| ≈ 1.00 in
lung, 1.04–1.14 in marrow). The shared space maps old cells into a
**frozen young** reference; that can pull old cells toward the young
manifold and hide real change. If so, a space learned from **both**
ages on a common scale should show change the shared space does not.

## Inputs (on disk in your folder)

`benchmarks/aging/data/{lung,marrow}_processed.h5ad` (A01) and
`benchmarks/aging/{lung,marrow}_shared.h5ad` (A02); check sha256 against
the A01/A02 reports first.

## Steps

All scVI fits: exactly the A02 settings (`n_latent=15`, `n_layers=2`,
NB, `batch_key="donor_id"`, CPU, `max_epochs=400`, early stopping
patience 30). All drift fits: exactly A02 (`n_epochs=1500`, drift
`seed=0`, `beta=1.0`, `lr=5e-4`, ESM exact, locked defaults), then
`classify_basin_latent(rule="lead_eig")`, tol = 0.1·std(lead_re).

1. **Joint space** — `scripts/x04_joint_fit.py`: one scVI on young + old
   together, **age not a covariate**, scVI seeds 0, 1, 2. Drift on each
   joint latent.
2. **Shared-space seed replicates** — `scripts/x04_shared_seeds.py`:
   repeat A02's reference (young) + scArches query (old) mapping with
   scVI seeds 1 and 2 (seed 0 is A02's; reuse it, do not refit).
   Drift on each. This gives the seed-to-seed noise of every quantity.
3. **Per-type quantities** — `scripts/x04_compare.py`, for each of the
   8 eligible types, each space (`shared` s0–s2, `joint` s0–s2):
   * `stiff_ratio` = old/young `median_abs_lambda` of the centroid
     operators (`compare.centroid_operator`, 30 cells);
   * `aniso_ratio` = old/young anisotropy (A03 definition);
   * `spread_ratio` = old/young trace of the latent covariance of the
     type's cells;
   * `offset_z` = ‖old centroid − young centroid‖ ÷ sqrt(trace of the
     young covariance) — a scale-free displacement;
   * `basin_fraction_young`, `basin_fraction_old`;
   * the frozen tests `compare_types(...)` with A03's exact arguments
     (`iso_pass`, `ant_pass`, `S`, `iso_null_q95`), reported as
     exploratory.
   Write `benchmarks/aging/x04/per_type.csv` (one row per space × seed
   × tissue × type) and `benchmarks/aging/x04/summary.csv` (per space ×
   type: mean and min–max over the three seeds of each quantity).
4. **Reading rule (fixed before the run; apply mechanically)** — per
   type, using seed means and the seed range:
   * the type **shows hidden softening** if joint `stiff_ratio` ≤ 0.90
     **and** shared `stiff_ratio` ≥ 0.95 **and** the joint and shared
     seed ranges do not overlap;
   * **shows hidden displacement** if joint `offset_z` exceeds the
     shared `offset_z` by more than the larger of the two seed ranges;
   * verdict for the arm: **"mapping hides change"** if ≥ 2 of the 3
     basin types (lung fibroblast, lung NK, marrow granulocyte) or ≥ 4
     of all 8 types show hidden softening or hidden displacement;
     otherwise **"no evidence that mapping hides change"**.
   Write the verdict string to the report metric `x04_verdict` and the
   per-type flags to `summary.csv`.
5. **Figures** (`benchmarks/figures/aging/x04/`, PDF + PNG, footer
   "EXPLORATORY · aging/X04 · not part of the frozen outcome"):
   `stiff_ratio` shared vs joint per type with seed ranges as error
   bars and the 0.90 / 0.95 lines; `offset_z` likewise.
6. `benchmarks/aging/x04/RESULT_X04.md`: question, what ran, the reading
   rule verbatim, per-type table, verdict. No interpretation beyond the
   rule; Claude interprets in review.

## Gate

* Frozen-reference check for the seed-1/2 shared mappings (latent
  encoder parameters **and** BatchNorm buffers change by 0), as A02.
* `sym_max` ≤ 1e-4 for every drift fit.
* Otherwise none: every verdict is a result.
