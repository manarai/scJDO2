# aging/X05 — Per-mouse uncertainty (EXPLORATORY)

**Exploratory.** Nothing here changes the frozen A03 outcome or
RESULT.md. State "EXPLORATORY" in the first line of your report. No
model is fitted in this task: everything is computed from stored
latents and per-cell Jacobians.

## Question

The §4b/§5 nulls split *young cells*, pooled across mice, so they
capture cell-sampling noise but not differences between mice. Old and
young are different mice. X04 left one robust effect:
**granulocytopoietic cells stiffen with age** (old/young median |λ|
×1.5–6.9 across seeds and spaces). Is it larger than mouse-to-mouse
variation? And how stable are the A03 test calls when one old mouse is
left out?

## Inputs (on disk in your folder; check sha256 against A02/X04 reports)

* Primary: `benchmarks/aging/{lung,marrow}_shared.h5ad` (A02, scVI seed
  0): `X_scVI_shared`, per-cell `J_latent`, `donor_id`, `age_group`,
  `cell_type`.
* Seed replicates: the X04 shared-space seed 1 and 2 h5ad files (both
  tissues), for step 2 only.

## Steps (`scripts/x05_donor_uncertainty.py`, one script)

1. **Per-mouse operators** (primary seed 0). For each eligible type and
   each mouse with **≥ 30 cells** of that type: centroid operator =
   `compare.centroid_operator(z, J, 30)` on that mouse's cells. Record
   `median_abs_lambda`, `lead_eig`, `anisotropy` (A03 definitions), and
   `offset_z` = ‖mouse centroid − pooled-young centroid‖ ÷
   sqrt(trace of the pooled-young covariance). Mice below 30 cells are
   listed and excluded for that type. →
   `benchmarks/aging/x05/per_mouse.csv`.
2. **Exact permutation test across mice**, per type, on
   `log(median_abs_lambda)` per mouse: statistic
   `T = mean(old mice) − mean(young mice)`; null = every relabelling of
   which mice are young, keeping the number of young mice fixed
   (marrow: C(12, 2) = 66; lung: C(n, n_young) over mice passing the
   30-cell floor). Report `T`, the number of relabellings, and
   **one-sided** `p_stiffer = P(T_perm ≥ T)` and
   `p_softer = P(T_perm ≤ T)`. Repeat on the X04 shared seeds 1 and 2.
   → `benchmarks/aging/x05/mouse_permutation.csv`.
3. **Leave-one-old-mouse-out** (primary seed 0): for each tissue and each
   old mouse, drop that mouse's cells and rerun `compare_types` with
   A03's exact arguments (`use_rep="X_scVI_shared"`, 30, 200, 1 000
   nulls, seed 0). A type falling below 200 cells per group is `NA` for
   that drop, not rescued. Also record the pooled old/young
   `median_abs_lambda` ratio per drop. →
   `benchmarks/aging/x05/loo_old.csv`
   (tissue, dropped_mouse, cell_type, iso_pass, ant_pass, S,
   iso_null_q95, stiff_ratio).
4. **Reading rule (fixed before the run; apply mechanically).**
   * Granulocytopoietic stiffening **exceeds mouse-to-mouse variation**
     if all three hold: `p_stiffer ≤ 0.05` in seed 0; `p_stiffer ≤ 0.05`
     in at least one of seeds 1, 2; and the leave-one-out `stiff_ratio`
     stays ≥ 1.5 for every dropped old mouse. Otherwise **"not
     distinguishable from mouse-to-mouse variation"**.
   * For every type, report the fraction of leave-one-out drops in which
     `iso_pass` and `ant_pass` equal the A03 call ("call stability").
   Write `x05_verdict` and the per-type stability to
   `benchmarks/aging/x05/summary.csv`.
5. **Figures** (`benchmarks/figures/aging/x05/`, PDF + PNG, footer
   "EXPLORATORY · aging/X05 · not part of the frozen outcome"):
   per-type strip plot of per-mouse `median_abs_lambda` (log y), young
   and old side by side, one point per mouse, with the permutation `p`
   annotated; leave-one-out stability heatmap (types × dropped mice,
   cell = iso/ant call).
6. `benchmarks/aging/x05/RESULT_X05.md`: question, what ran, the rule
   verbatim, tables, verdict. No interpretation beyond the rule.
