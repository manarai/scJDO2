## Interpretation

**Result (frozen, PREREG §6).** Isotropy passes in 3/8 types, anticipation
in 1/8; neither is a majority, so the outcome row is
**isotropic noise increase; no aging claim beyond stiffness**.

**What the numbers show within that row (descriptive, not a test).**
1. *The label's "noise increase" is not seen in stiffness.* Median |λ|
   old/young in the shared space: lung 1.01 and 1.00; marrow
   1.04–1.14, granulocytopoietic 3.1. Nothing got uniformly softer; the
   marrow operators are, if anything, slightly stiffer in old mice. So
   the honest reading of this row here is **no detectable aging change
   in operator shape, and no stiffness loss**, not "basins widened
   uniformly". The header stays verbatim; this qualifies it.
2. *The true basins do not change.* The three types that are basins by
   the locked rule (lung fibroblast, lung NK, marrow granulocyte) fail
   both tests, with lead eigenvalue and stiffness within ±4 % of young.
3. *The isotropy passes are all in marrow transit states*
   (granulocytopoietic cell, hematopoietic precursor, precursor B),
   none of which is a basin (holder fraction ≤ 0.44). 3 of 8 passes is
   more than chance would give (≈ 0.6 % if the tests were independent),
   so something direction-specific changes there; in granulocytopoietic
   cells the lead eigenvalue goes from +0.51 (unstable) to −1.56
   (stable) and the holder fraction from 0.20 to 0.44, i.e. the state
   becomes *more* basin-like in old marrow. This is a hypothesis, not a
   pre-registered claim.
4. *The anticipation pass is marginal and consistent with chance*:
   promonocyte |cos| 0.470 vs null q95 0.463; ≈ 34 % chance of ≥ 1 pass
   in 8 under the null.

**Caveats.**
* Mapping old cells into a frozen young reference can pull them toward
  the young manifold, which biases toward "no change". Standalone fits
  hint at lung softening (fibroblast old/young median |λ| 0.67, NK
  0.88), but standalone latents have independent scales, so those
  ratios are not comparable; this is a reason to check, not a finding.
* Lung: 2 types, reference trained on 648 cells. Marrow: 2 young
  donors. One scVI seed.
* `lambda_ratio_soft_axis` is unstable (−15.9 to +26.5) for non-basin
  types because their soft eigenvalue sits near zero; do not read it
  there.

**Manus's observations.** All hold up. Its two suggestions (basin-only
subset; donor-level uncertainty) are adopted as proposals X03 and X05.
