## Interpretation (exploratory; the frozen A03 outcome is unchanged)
1. **The mapping does not hide change.** Old–young offsets are
   *smaller* in the joint space than in the shared space for every type,
   the opposite of shrinkage; no type softens by ≥ 10 % in the joint
   space. Lung NK is the closest (0.93, range 0.84–0.97), below the
   rule's threshold.
2. **The A03 isotropy passes are not robust to the scVI seed.** Of
   A03's three passes, HPC passes in 1/3 shared seeds and 0/3 joint;
   pre-B 2/3 and 0/3; granulocytopoietic 2/3 and 1/3. Granulocyte, which
   failed in A03, passes in another seed. The direction-specific
   "transit-state" lead from A03 is therefore fragile and should not be
   carried forward as a finding.
3. **One effect is robust: granulocytopoietic cells get stiffer with
   age** — stiffness ratio > 1.5 in every seed of both spaces (shared
   3.1–6.9, joint 1.5–3.6). It is a magnitude change, not a
   direction-specific one.
4. **Seed noise is large relative to the effects.** Lung fibroblast's
   shared stiffness ratio ranges 1.01–1.18 across seeds; A03's single-
   seed numbers carry roughly ±10 % uncertainty from the embedding
   alone.
