## Interpretation
1. **scVelo fails on realistic kinetics.** Continuous transcription change
   with fast splicing and slow degradation is outside scVelo's switching
   model; its gene velocities carry no sign information here and its
   embedding reverses development. Labelling, from the same cells, is
   right (0.896; 0.944 on moving axes).
2. **The ranking protects measured flow, but the filler hurts.** Cells
   labelling decides are untouched (by construction); velocity then fills
   the cells labelling does not reach with mostly wrong signs, so
   `{labelling, velocity}` drops to 0.665 while coverage rises
   0.63 → 0.83. Flow–developmental disagreement is 43 % with raw labelling.
3. **Palantir pseudotime works on realistic data**: 0.913 against the
   developmental truth (0.973 on moving axes), far above the 0.69 on the
   analytic generator that motivated §13. Caveat: its start cell was
   chosen from the truth (best case).
4. **Lineage and pseudotime are both good; their conflicts are hard
   cells** (lineage right in 66 % of them). Combined coverage 0.83 misses
   the 0.90 gate.

## Proposed next tasks (for the user)
* **Dataset-level reliability check for the velocity source**: where
  labelling and velocity both sign axes, if their agreement falls below a
  fixed threshold (e.g. 0.6), demote velocity to consistency-check only for
  that dataset and say so in the ledger. Here it would have kept flow at
  labelling's 0.896 instead of 0.665.
* **Revise §13**: pseudotime meets 0.90 on realistic data with a good root;
  test the same with a non-oracle root (earliest sampling day only) before
  restoring any gate.

# Correction (2026-10-17, after a data audit; supersedes interpretation 1 above)

The cause of scVelo's failure was stated wrongly as "realistic kinetics".
An audit of the S01 generator (`coord/orient/reviews/audit_2026-10-17/`)
found:

* **The data is computed correctly** (observed counts match the true pools
  exactly, pure Poisson), **but splicing is unrealistic**: unspliced is
  2.1 % of counts (real 10x data ~15–30 %), 94.7 % of unspliced entries are
  zero; no gene switches or reaches a steady state within the 4 days.
* **The direction is in the data:** with kNN-30 moments and the *true*
  rates, the splicing estimate has median cosine +0.29 with the truth
  (+0.58 at 17 % unspliced).
* **Rate estimation reverses it:** with γ fitted from the phase portrait
  (steady-state extreme-quantile fit, as scVelo), median cosine −0.15
  (−0.36 at 17 % unspliced). Without switching genes or steady states, the
  portrait identifies biased rates. Labelling avoids this (its pulse is a
  clock).

So S03 is a test of scVelo on data built against its assumptions, not on
realistic data. The S03 numbers stand as measured; their meaning is
limited to this generator. Generator fixed in orient/S05; S02–S04 rerun on
it afterwards.
