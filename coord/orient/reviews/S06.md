## Interpretation (Claude)
1. **Negative control — the §16 guard is too weak.** 104 of 180 day-4-root
   runs were accepted: Spearman(pseudotime, day) ranges −0.75…+0.54 for
   day-4 roots and +0.33…+0.84 for day-1 roots, so no correlation
   threshold separates them. The **actual start cell's day** does: every
   day-4 run starts on day 3–4; 2 default-mode day-1 runs were snapped by
   Palantir to a day-4 boundary cell. Rule "pseudotime minimum on the
   earliest sampling day" (checked post hoc on these 360 runs) refuses all
   180 day-4 runs and 8 day-1 runs (whose agreement was 0.89–0.96 —
   conservative). Derived from this data → must be confirmed on new runs.
2. **Lineage is weaker than pseudotime under sparse sampling.** Lineage
   0.72–0.74 on 18 % of cells; pseudotime 0.96 on 80 %. Because lineage
   outranks pseudotime, `{lineage, pseudotime}` (0.91–0.93) is worse than
   pseudotime alone. Same cause as the sparse chord truth (S06 R0 review):
   most clones have one sampled cell at an end. The trust ranking assumed
   dense lineage.
3. **The score-matching J does not detect the dynamical fork** on this
   developmental time series (AUC 0.18; committed cells' lead eigenvalue
   +0.28, saddle +0.02). In nearly every cell, v1 and v2 data alike and
   even `stationary_cycle` (no fork), J has exactly one small positive
   eigenvalue (≈ +0.2…+0.6) against strongly negative ones (≈ −5): the
   curvature of a density concentrated on a curved trajectory, not the
   dynamics. **Claude, one dataset (v2_default s0, PCA):** the Jacobian of
   `MeasuredDrift` fitted on the raw labelling velocity gives saddle +0.095,
   committed +0.019, **AUC 0.996** (truth: +0.129 / −0.013, AUC 1.000).
4. **scVelo** on v2 is still reversed on 2 of 3 seeds (0.19, 0.26) and
   demoted every time; labelling sits at the gate (0.82–0.87; 0.90–0.94 on
   moving axes).

## Proposals (for the user)
* Pseudotime guard: require the pseudotime minimum on the earliest sampling
  day (§16 amendment); confirm on fresh runs.
* Developmental ranking: lineage only from clones with ≥ 2 cells at both
  ends, or a dataset-level reliability check between lineage and
  pseudotime; decide which.
* Fork/basin from dynamics: add a flow operator (Jacobian of the measured
  drift fitted on labelling velocity) beside the score-matching J; test
  across all v2 datasets with the same truth classes.
