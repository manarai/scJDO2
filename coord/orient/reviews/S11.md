## Interpretation (Claude)
1. **scNT-seq: the velocity sources disagree before any operator is
   fitted** — even our labelling velocity and dynamo's labelling velocity
   from the same new/total counts (−0.05). Operator disagreement there is
   inherited. Likely causes: different velocity definitions (ours: total
   RNA, `α̂ − γ·total`; dynamo's `velocity_alpha_minus_gamma_s` on its
   normalised spliced/new layers, mapped here by a raw-count chain rule —
   possibly wrong units), different γ estimation, scVelo's 72 genes. Until
   resolved, labelling-based flow J on real data is unvalidated.
2. **LARRY: independent velocities agree (0.70) but per-cell Jacobians do
   not** (at null level). Turning velocity into a derivative is the weak
   step: the OT operator is stable across seeds and estimators (0.93–0.95),
   the lineage one rests on 1 321 cells and extrapolates elsewhere. The
   null itself reaches 0.27, so part of the λ₁ pattern follows where cells
   are, not the velocity — the null should control for geometry.
3. **Coarse agreement:** both LARRY flows rank progenitors above terminal
   types — consistent with transitions in progenitors; per-cell
   resolution is not supported yet.
4. Score vs flow differ everywhere (developing systems, consistent with
   non-equilibrium).
