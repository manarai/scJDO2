## Interpretation
1. **Two truths, one sign.** Lineage measures developmental direction
   (where a clone went over days) and matches that truth at 0.98–0.99.
   Labelling (and velocity) measure the instantaneous flow. They diverge
   where trajectories bend or slow, and there the merge's ranking
   (lineage > labelling) imposes the wrong kind of answer: when they
   conflict, truth sides with labelling 70–81 % of the time. Pseudotime is
   also an ordering, which plausibly explains its earlier failure against
   an instantaneous truth.
2. **Slow cells are hard for every source.** All agreements fall as cells
   settle (day 4).
3. **MeasuredDrift smoothing lowers sign agreement** (0.896 → 0.849) while
   raising coverage (0.63 → 0.73).
4. **PCA beats FA** in every preset and source.
5. `stationary_cycle`: labelling agreement 0.97, flags correct.
