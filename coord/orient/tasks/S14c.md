# orient/S14c — S14b with an extended reserve

**Everything in `coord/orient/tasks/S14b.md` (including Amendment 1) applies
unchanged** — configuration, seed roles, eligibility criteria (a)–(d), the
commit order, gates X1–X4, outputs — except the three points below.

## Disclosure (planner)
S14b fixed the transition reserve at s5–s16 and said not to widen it. Only 2
of 4 seeds were eligible, and **no model had been fitted on any seed**.
Eligibility is decided from truth alone, before fitting, so extending the
reserve cannot bias the model test. This change is the planner's, made after
S14b's construction failure and stated here openly.

## Changes
1. **Reserve:** `v3_transition` **s17–s40**, traversed in order after S14b's
   eligible **s12, s16** (reuse them and their committed eligibility rows;
   do not regenerate). Stop generating once **2 more** seeds are eligible
   (4 in total). Fewer than 4 after s40: report a construction failure and stop.
2. **Generator guard** (any v3 generation in this task): one attempt per
   seed; stop a generation exceeding **60 min wall or 12 GB RSS** and record
   it as `guard_stop`; a generator exception is retried once, then recorded
   as `generator_error`. Guarded or failed seeds count as ineligible and
   stay in the table.
3. **Paths:** outputs under `benchmarks/synthetic_multimodal/s14c/`; script
   `scripts/s14c_confirm.py`, importing `scripts/s14b_confirm.py` (do not
   modify it; parametrise reserve and output paths in the new script).
   Null seeds (`v3_nofork` s3–s10 calibration, s11–s13 held-out) as S14b;
   reuse the already generated/prepared `v3_nofork_s3`.
