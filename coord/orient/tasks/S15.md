## Steps (`scripts/s15_archetypes.py`)

1. **Descriptors — magnitude-free** (S14: values not recovered). Per cell and
   operator, from `scjdo2.opspace.spectrum`/`invariants` (operator stored as
   `uns["scjdo2"]["J"]` on a copy): sign of Re λ₁; number of eigenvalues with
   Re > 0; Re λ₁ / max|λ|; max|Im| / max|λ|; spectral gap / max|λ|; Henrici
   index; transient gain; trace / Σ|λ|. Then each descriptor
   **rank-transformed within dataset** (quantiles). Raw-magnitude descriptors
   computed too, report only.
2. **Operators:** `J_flow*` and score J. Score J: S10's `J_score` for the
   same dataset when it exists in the PCA-15 space; otherwise fit with S10's
   score settings in PCA-15 (record which). dynamo is **not** in S15 (S16).
3. **Archetypes (two views):** (i) `scjdo2.archetypes.ArchetypeDecomposer`
   on per-cell operators normalised to unit Frobenius norm, K by its held-out
   rule over (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8); dominant archetype per cell. (ii) K-means on
   the rank descriptors, K from the same candidates by best mean silhouette
   over 5 restarts.
4. **Baseline without operators:** Leiden on expression PCA-15, resolution
   searched to give the matched K.
5. **Scores:** NMI/ARI with truth regimes; per regime best-matching archetype
   recall/precision; `saddle` precision ÷ base rate; median descriptors per
   archetype; on the cycle preset, whether an archetype is defined by high
   max|Im|/max|λ| (> 0.5 × the truth operator's).
6. **Stability:** ARI of assignments across drift seeds 0–2 and across
   k = 10 / 15 / 20.
