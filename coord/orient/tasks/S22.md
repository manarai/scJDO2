# orient/S22 — Score-J stability vs clonal fate coherence (LARRY)

Agent **B** (Manus cloud sandbox; bundle in, bundle out; never push; never
use the user's Mac). 

## Claim tested

Score J = Hessian of log p (symmetric) describes how strongly a state holds
its cells. If so, a clone whose cells sit where the landscape is **stiff**
(λ₁ of score J strongly negative) should keep its sisters in one fate at
the next time point; a clone in a **shallow or saddle** region (λ₁ near or
above 0) should spread over fates. Lineage barcodes are the truth; no
velocity, OT or flow J is used.

## Steps (`scripts/s22_score_lineage.py`)

1. **Score J** — S11's score fit exactly (`scripts/s11_operators.py::run_score`
   settings: `EnergyDriftAdapter(30, EnergyDriftConfig(hidden=96, depth=3,
   n_epochs=1500, seed=s, batch_size=256, loss="esm", lr=5e-4, beta=1.0,
   esm_laplacian="exact"))`) on `z_all[sample_index]`, seeds s = 0, 1, 2
   (separate fits; gates use seed 0). Symmetry check as S11 (max_asym ≤ 1e-4).
   Evaluate the Jacobian by autodiff at **every barcoded day-2 and day-4
   cell**; per cell λ₁ = largest eigenvalue (`eigvalsh`), and also the
   number of eigenvalues > 0 (report only).
2. **Clones** — for t ∈ {2, 4}: clones with ≥ 2 cells on day t and ≥ 3
   cells on day t + 2. Per clone: predictor `λ₁_clone` = median λ₁ of its
   day-t cells; truth `H` = Shannon entropy (natural log) of the cell-type
   distribution of its day-(t + 2) cells; also `switch` = fraction of
   day-(t + 2) cells whose type differs from the clone's day-t majority type
   (report only).
3. **Baselines (inputs only)** — per day-t cell: local density = −log of the
   mean distance to its 30 nearest day-t neighbours in `z_all`; distance to
   the day-2 `Undifferentiated` centroid. Same clone median.
4. **Scores** — Spearman ρ(λ₁_clone, H) pooled over t = 2, 4 and per t;
   within the `Undifferentiated` stratum (clones whose day-t majority type is
   `Undifferentiated`); partial Spearman controlling for local density.
   **Null:** 1 000 permutations of `λ₁_clone` among clones of the same t and
   same day-t majority type (`default_rng(0)`); one-sided p (ρ > 0) with
   +1 correction.

## Gates (fixed now; seed 0)

* **L1** pooled ρ(λ₁_clone, H) > 0 with permutation p < 0.01.
* **L2** within the `Undifferentiated` stratum ρ > 0 with p < 0.01 (the effect
  is not only "progenitors vs differentiated").
* **L3** within the `Undifferentiated` stratum |ρ| of λ₁ exceeds |ρ| of each
  baseline (density, distance to centroid), and the partial ρ given density
  stays > 0.

Everything else (seeds 1–2, per-t, switch, eigen-count, figures) report only.
No tuning: the settings above are fixed.
