## Idea

At steady state a basin's curvature sets its response: perturbations
displace cells most along **soft** directions (small curvature), and the
fluctuation–response relation ties this to the covariance. Fit score J on
**control cells only**; predict the directions of perturbation-induced
shifts; check against perturbed cells the fit never saw. For a single
Gaussian basin, score-J soft axes equal the control covariance axes, so the
honest baselines are the **global and local control covariance** — score J
must be compared with them, not only with chance.

## Steps (`scripts/s18_perturb_response.py`)

1. **Embedding fitted on controls only:** library-normalise (median of
   controls), `log1p`, 2 000 HVGs (scanpy `seurat_v3` on control counts),
   PCA-k with k ∈ {10, 15, 20, 30} fitted on controls; project every
   perturbed cell with the same transform.
2. **Perturbations analysed:** targets with ≥ 100 cells; effect test =
   energy distance between the target's cells and 2 000 random controls in
   PCA-20, permutation p (200 permutations, `default_rng(0)`); keep
   targets with BH-adjusted p < 0.01 (report how many).
3. **Score J on controls:** `EnergyDriftAdapter` (S02 configuration) on the
   control cells in PCA-k; per control cell `H = J_score`; per target the
   **local** operator = mean `H` over the 200 control cells nearest the
   target's centroid (in PCA-k), and the **global** operator = mean over all
   controls.
4. **Observed shift:** `Δμ` = target centroid − centroid of its 200 nearest
   controls (PCA-k).
5. **Predicted response subspace (m = 3):** the m softest directions of
   each operator (eigenvectors of `−H` with the smallest positive
   eigenvalues; if fewer than m positive, the m smallest |eigenvalues|).
   **Capture** = `‖P Δμ‖² / ‖Δμ‖²`.
6. **Baselines, same m:** top-m eigenvectors of the global control
   covariance; of the local covariance (same 200 controls); random m-dim
   subspaces (1 000 draws → null distribution, expectation m/k).
7. Also report m ∈ {1, 5}, all k, and per-target results with the target's
   cell count and effect size.

## Gate (fixed now; k = 20, m = 3)

* **P1** local score-J capture exceeds the 95th percentile of its random
  null for ≥ 70 % of the significant targets.
* Report only (no gate): score J local vs local covariance vs global
  covariance — the median paired difference in capture across targets, with
  a Wilcoxon signed-rank p. State plainly whichever wins.
