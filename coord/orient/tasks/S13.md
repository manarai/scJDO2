# orient/S13 — Whitened flow J across embeddings

## Hypothesis

PCA and FA are linear projections of the same data and see the same flow
(S12: OT velocities agree in gene space, median cos 0.52–0.56), but our
velocity→Jacobian pipeline depends on axis scaling (Euclidean OT cost, fixed
drift regularisation). **Whitening each embedding by its own data
covariance** should remove that dependence: for linearly related embeddings
whitened coordinates differ by a rotation, and Jacobian eigenvalues are
rotation-invariant. Test whether whitened OT flow J then detects the fork in
every linear embedding. No other change to the pipeline.

**Never use a truth key as an input** (S06/S10 list; `z_true`, `fate_true`,
`jacobian_state`, true velocities, `core_dims`): truth only for scoring.
Full **synthetic** datasets, no cell subsampling (they must equal S09/S10).
**LARRY (amended, Revision 1):** use S11's fixed sample of 4 000 cells per
time point (`default_rng(0)`), exactly as S10 Step 5 — the full data
(28 249 / 48 498 / 54 140 cells) would need ≈ 39 GiB for the dense day 4 → 6
OT matrices, over the memory stop. The no-subsampling rule applies to the
synthetic datasets only.

## Data and embeddings (read; reuse S10/S12 outputs; never modify inputs)

`v3_transition` seeds 0–2 (**primary**, ≈ 350 saddle cells each) and
`v3_default` seeds 0–2 (report only; 22–29 saddle cells) from
`MANIFEST_v3.csv`; embeddings **PCA-10, PCA-20, PCA-50, FA-20** as built in
S12 (`scripts/s12_step0.py`; import, do not edit); scVI-10 from S12 report
only. LARRY (S11 paths, PCA-30 and FA-30 as S10 Step 5) for the real-data
part.

## Method (`scripts/s13_whitened.py`)

For each dataset × embedding `z` (n × d):
1. **Whitening:** `μ` = mean of `z`, `Σ` = covariance of `z` over all cells,
   eigendecomposition `Σ = V Λ Vᵀ` with eigenvalues floored at
   `1e-6 · max(Λ)`; `W = V Λ^{-1/2} Vᵀ`; `z̃ = (z − μ) W`.
2. **OT velocity in z̃:** balanced POT `ot.sinkhorn` between consecutive
   sampling days, ε = 0.05 × median squared distance **in z̃**, barycentric
   velocity / Δt for earlier-day cells (S10's code path, applied to z̃).
3. **Flow operator in z̃:** `MeasuredDrift(d, seed=0)` defaults fitted on
   `(z̃, ṽ)`; `J̃ = jacobian_autodiff(z̃)`. Leading real eigenvalue λ₁ from
   `J̃` (equal to that of `J = W⁻¹ J̃ W` in original coordinates).
4. **Two partial variants, report only:** whitened OT + drift fitted in raw
   `z` (velocity mapped back, `v = ṽ W⁻¹`); raw OT + drift fitted in `z̃`.
5. **Scores (truth, scoring only):** fork AUC of λ₁ (saddle vs committed,
   S06 Step 4 classes); per-cell Spearman of λ₁ between every pair of
   embeddings; fate-axis |cos| at saddle cells (axis from S12).
6. **Truth-free embedding choice (report only):** on each `v3_transition`
   seed and on LARRY — fit the whitened flow on the first day pair only,
   integrate the drift from the middle-day cells for the gap (Euler,
   Δt = 1 h), map predicted and observed last-day cells to gene space by
   the embedding's linear map (pseudo-inverse), project both onto one
   common reference (PCA-50 of the log-normalised data, fitted on all
   cells), and compute the energy distance; baselines: no movement, mean
   displacement. Report which embedding predicts best.

## Gate (fixed now)

**Whitened OT flow J fork AUC ≥ 0.85 on every `v3_transition` seed in at
least 3 of the 4 linear embeddings** (PCA-10, PCA-20, PCA-50, FA-20).
Everything else is report only. A failed gate is a result.
