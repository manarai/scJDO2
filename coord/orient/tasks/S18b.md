## Embedding

S18's control-only PCA (k = 20 primary; 15 and 30 report).

## Step 1 — control states from score J (`scripts/s18b_occupancy.py`)

* Score J = S18's `EnergyDriftAdapter` on controls (reuse the k = 20 fit).
* **Basins:** from every control cell follow the fitted drift `f = ∇ log p`
  (Euler, step 0.05, 400 steps, stop when ‖step‖ < 1e-4); cluster the
  endpoints (agglomerative, distance threshold = 0.5 × median
  nearest-neighbour distance of endpoints); each cluster is a basin; basins
  with < 100 control cells are merged into the nearest basin by endpoint
  distance. Record the number of basins.
* **Depth per basin:** (a) **stiffness** = median over its cells of
  `−λ_max(H)` restricted to the top-3 local data directions (S12 tangent
  rule: top-3 eigenvectors of the cell's kNN-30 covariance) — larger = deeper;
  (b) **energy depth** = median energy of the basin's cells minus the
  energy at its endpoint minimum. Report both; (a) is primary.
* **Comparison states (no operator):** Leiden on the control PCA at the
  resolution giving the same number of states.

## Step 2 — occupancy per knockdown

Assign every cell (controls and the 865 significant targets) to a basin by
its nearest control cell (k = 15 vote) in PCA-20. Occupancy log-fold-change
per target and basin: `log2((n_tb + 1)/(N_t + B)) − log2((n_cb + 1)/(N_c + B))`.
**Sensitivity of a basin** = median over targets of |log2 FC|.

## Step 3 — tests

* **H1 (re-weighting premise):** for each target, fit non-negative weights
  on the basins so the re-weighted control population best matches the
  target's cells (minimise energy distance in PCA-20; weights on basins,
  cells within a basin uniform). **Explained fraction** = 1 − E(target,
  re-weighted controls) / E(target, controls). Noise floor: the same
  statistic for 200 random control subsets of the target's size.
* **H2 (depth predicts sensitivity):** Spearman over basins between depth
  (stiffness) and sensitivity; **partial** Spearman controlling for basin
  size (number of control cells) and basin spread (trace of its control
  covariance); permutation p (10 000 permutations of depth across basins).
  Same for energy depth, and for Leiden states with their covariance-based
  "depth" (1 / trace) as the operator-free comparison.
* Shifts for any centroid statistic are measured against **all** controls
  (S18 review: nearest-control matching removes the within-cloud component).

## Gates (fixed now; k = 20)

* **H1** median explained fraction ≥ 0.5 over the 865 targets, and above
  the 95th percentile of the noise floor for ≥ 50 % of targets.
* **H2** partial Spearman(stiffness, sensitivity | size, spread) < 0 with
  permutation p < 0.05.

Everything else report only (energy depth, Leiden comparison, k = 15 / 30,
per-target tables). State plainly if the operator-free comparison does as
well.

## Amendment — Revision 1 (planner, 2026-10-22; fixed before any result)

1. **Leiden comparison:** its depth (1 / trace of control covariance) is a
   monotone function of the spread it would be adjusted for, so its partial
   Spearman is undefined by construction — report it as **null**. Report
   the Leiden **ordinary** Spearman(1 / trace, sensitivity) with its
   permutation p: the operator-free question "does spread alone predict
   sensitivity?". The score-J tests (H1, H2 including the partial Spearman
   controlling for size and spread) are unchanged.
2. **Permutation tail:** **one-sided for negative association** (shallow
   basins more sensitive — the direction fixed by the hypothesis);
   `p = (1 + #{perm ρ ≤ observed ρ}) / (1 + 10 000)`. The gate uses this p;
   also report the two-sided p.
3. **Refit check:** the network weights were not saved in S18, so refit with
   S18's exact code path and configuration (permitted). Before using it,
   compare its Jacobians at the control cells with S18's cached `H_k20`:
   report the median relative Frobenius difference; if it exceeds 0.05, stop
   and report.
4. **Tangent directions** for stiffness: S12's executable rule as found
   (k = 30 neighbours, neighbourhood-mean centred, covariance denominator
   29), top **3** eigenvectors.
