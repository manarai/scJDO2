## Step 0 — transition preset (generator; amended before issue)

The calibrated v3 design samples at 48, 96, 144 h, but the pitchfork is
crossed at `z1 = 1` (≈ 30 h): every sampled cell is already past it, so a
transition test is degenerate (pilot: the true transition fell in the first
pseudotime bin on every seed). Add a config field `z1_crit` (default 1.0 —
v1/v2/v3 unchanged, test it) used everywhere the constant is (pitchfork
`μ`, cycle exit gate, fate label, switching genes' fate gate), and a preset
**`v3_transition`** = `v3_default` knobs with `z1_crit = 2.0` (μ crosses 0
at ≈ 82 h: day 2 before, day 4 just after, day 6 committed). Generate seeds
0–2 (MANIFEST_v3 rows); run S08's statistics on them (report: does moving
the fork change realism). Steps 1–4 run on `v3_transition` in addition to
the other v3 presets; **the transition gates use `v3_transition`**.

## Step 0b — labelling realism additions (2026-10-19; `coord/orient/reviews/labelling_diag_2026-10-19/`)

On real scNT-seq the labelling velocity's direction depended on the γ
assumption and on smoothing. So in this task: (i) `v3_transition` also gets
a **pulse-group batch effect** — the 3 h and 5 h cells of each day get
separate per-gene capture factors `LogNormal(0, 0.3)` (seeded, stored as
truth), as in scNT-seq, where pulses are separate batches; (ii) the
labelling velocity is computed in **four variants**: raw or kNN-30-smoothed
counts (normalised moments, as dynamo) × γ shared across pulses
(`LabelledRateModel`) or one γ per pulse (refit per pulse group). Every
labelling-based operator and score below is reported per variant; the
**primary** variant is smoothed + shared γ.

`exp(Δt · log 2 / 16 h)` adjusted for sampling — scoring-side knowledge, used
only for this sensitivity) and `reg_m ∈ {0.1, 1, 10}` × ε.

## Step 4 — critical transitions (`scripts/s10_transitions.py`)

Per dataset × embedding × operator (`J`, `J_flow`, `J_flow_scvelo`, `J_flow_ot`):

* **Signals per cell:** leading real eigenvalue `λ₁`; curvature along the
  **data-derived** soft axis `a` = the leading eigenvector of the operator
  averaged over the 10 % of cells with the largest `λ₁` (sign-aligned);
  `aᵀ J a`; local kNN-30 variance along `a`. Baseline: the critical-state
  index (Mojtahedi et al. 2016) on the top-200 HVGs.
* **Ordering without truth:** cells binned into 12 equal-count bins of
  Palantir pseudotime (day-2 default-mode root r0); per bin the median of
  each signal. The bin where a signal peaks is its **detected transition**.
* **Truth (scoring only):** the true transition bin = the bin where the
  median truth fate eigenvalue `κ(μ − 3 z0²)` peaks; also `μ` per bin.
* **Validity:** a dataset is informative for the transition test only if
  its true transition bin is in 2–9 (≥ 2 bins on each side); otherwise
  record it as `uninformative` — it counts neither as a pass nor a fail,
  and a gate with an uninformative seed fails as untestable.
* **Scores:** |detected − true bin| per signal; Spearman of the signal
  with bin index over the bins before the true transition (does it rise
  toward it).
* **Null:** `v3_stationary_cycle` (no transition): signals binned by a
  pseudotime from a random cell; report the peak-to-median ratio of each
  signal (should be ≈ 1).

## Step 6 — diffusion (`scripts/s10_diffusion.py`; added 2026-10-18)

The score is `drift / D`: score-operator magnitudes are divided by the
unknown diffusion `D`, which confounds basin-depth comparisons. Estimate `D`
in the embedding and score it against the truth.

* **Truth (generator, scoring only):** add `forward_displacement_covariance(adata,
  method, horizon_hours, n_paths=32, seed=0)` to the generator (new
  function; Step 0 rules): from each cell's true state and pools, simulate
  `n_paths` paths with the preset's SDE (all latent dims, nuisance
  programmes included) and RNA kinetics for `horizon_hours`; map
  expected-count positions through the stored embedding; return the
  covariance of the endpoints (`n × d × d`). **Effective diffusion**
  `D_eff(Δt) = Cov / (2 Δt)` — noise enters the latent state but reaches
  expression only through RNA turnover, so the reference is the spread over
  an interval, not σ. Horizons Δt ∈ {6, 24, 48} h. Summaries per cell:
  `trace(D_eff)/d` and `aᵀ D_eff a` along the Step 4 soft axis.
* **Measurement-noise reference:** regenerate the counts of each dataset
  from the same true pools with a different noise seed (Poisson/Gamma only),
  embed with the stored transform, and take the per-cell displacement
  covariance between the two draws / 2 → `Σ_meas`.
* **Estimators** (no truth):
  1. *score–flow*: in each cell's kNN-30 neighbourhood, the scalar `D̂` of
     least squares `f ≈ D̂ · s`, with `f` the labelling velocity and `s` the
     score (`obsm["X_drift_v"] / β`); and along the soft axis.
  2. *clone dispersion*: per clone with ≥ 3 cells at two consecutive time
     points, `(Cov_{t₂} − Cov_{t₁}) / (2 Δt)` minus `Σ_meas`, assigned to the
     clone's cells (drift divergence not removed — report).
  3. *OT noise level*: entropic OT between consecutive time points with
     `ε` on a grid (×0.25 … ×4 of the Step 1 value), chosen by held-out
     (20 % of the later time point, `rng(0)`) Sinkhorn divergence;
     `D̂ = ε̂ / (4 Δt)` for cost `|x − y|²` (Brownian kernel
     `exp(−|x−y|²/4DΔt)`), minus `trace(Σ_meas)/d` per unit.
* **Scores:** per estimator, ratio of median `D̂` to median truth
  `trace(D_eff)/d` (Δt matched: 48 h for 2 and 3, 6 h for 1); Spearman over
  cells (1, 2) or over day pairs (3); by truth class.
* **Payoff check:** `D̂ · J_score` vs `J_flow`: relative Frobenius
  distance and Spearman of `λ₁`, before and after scaling.
* Real data, report only: `larry` (estimators 2 and 3) and `scnt_hema`
  (estimator 1), with S11's embeddings and velocities.

## Gates (fixed now; `v3_default`, PCA, raw labelling, seeds 0–2)

* **Orientation:** `{labelling}` agreement ≥ 0.80; `{lineage, pseudotime}`
  agreement ≥ `{pseudotime}` − 0.01; independence exact; J unchanged.
* **Guard (fresh runs):** every latest-day-root run refused; ≥ 90 % of
  day-2-root runs accepted, each mode.
* **Flow operator:** `J_flow` AUC (saddle vs committed) ≥ 0.85 on every seed.
* **Critical transition** (`v3_transition`; every seed informative): with
  `J_flow` **and separately with `J_flow_ot`**, |detected − true bin| ≤ 1
  on every seed, and the null
  peak-to-median ratio of `λ₁` on `v3_stationary_cycle` is below its value
  at the detected transition on every seed. Also report the signed offset
  (detected − true bin): a consistent negative offset is an early warning
  only if the signal rises over ≥ 2 bins before it.

* **Diffusion** (`v3_default`, PCA, every seed): clone-dispersion and OT
  estimates each within a factor 2 of the truth's median effective
  diffusion (Δt = 48 h); score–flow estimate Spearman ≥ 0.5 with the
  truth over cells whose true state is a stable basin (`committed`).

Everything else (score `J`, scVelo-based flow J, FA, stress, batch, §17
sensitivity, lineage diagnostic, baseline, real data) is report only. A
failed gate is a result.
