# orient/S14b — Transition detection, confirmatory (one configuration)

## Why
In S14 (post hoc, 3 vs 3) the reference flow J separated transition from
no-fork data by peak-to-median R (transition 1.91 / 2.46 / 2.69; no-fork
1.44 / 0.58 / 1.19), but G2 was badly built: boundary-peaked truths were
scored, and the no-fork threshold depended on the weakest transition seed.
S14b tests the reference alone, on fresh seeds, with a corrected design.
**No factorial, no tuning, no alternative configuration.**

## Fixed (do not change)
* Generator v3 (`scripts/s10_fit_and_runs.py generate --preset P --seed N`),
  full datasets, MANIFEST_v3 rows + sha256.
* Preparation, PCA-15, strict S10 root rule and strict Palantir pseudotime
  exactly as S14 (`scripts/s14_choose_k.py` prepare / `_strict_pseudotime`).
* Flow J: S14 `reference` (`s14_test.config_for("reference")`, raw space,
  `last = "extrapolate"`), via `s14_choose_k.fit_flow`, drift seeds 0, 1, 2.
* Binning and R: S14's `day_weighted_bins` (12 bins) and S10's
  `peak_to_median` on per-bin median λ₁ (`s14_optimise.transition_score`).
  Also report P = max_b m_b − median_b m_b (report only).

## Seed allocation (frozen now; never reused for anything else)
| role | preset | seeds |
|---|---|---|
| null calibration | v3_nofork | s3–s10 (8) |
| held-out no-fork | v3_nofork | s11–s13 (3) |
| transition reserve | v3_transition | s5–s16, traversed in order |

## Step 1 — pre-model transition eligibility (truth only; before any fit on these seeds)
For each reserve seed in order: generate, prepare, strict pseudotime, then
the truth profile (`s14_optimise.transition_score`'s truth part: per-bin
median of 0.5·((z1 − z1_crit) − 3 z0²)). **Eligible** iff
(a) strict-root provenance complete; (b) every bin has ≥ 50 cells;
(c) true peak bin ∈ {2, …, 9}; (d) stable: in 200 bootstrap resamples of
cells within bins (`default_rng(seed)`), the argmax equals the true bin in
≥ 90 %. Write `g2_transition_eligibility.csv` (all reserve seeds, every
criterion) and **commit it before any flow fit on a transition seed**. Take
the first **4** eligible seeds; stop generating once 4 are found. If the
reserve gives fewer than 4: report a test-construction failure and stop
(do not widen the bins or the reserve).

## Step 2 — null calibration (fit only after Step 1 is committed)
Fit flow J on the 8 calibration no-fork seeds. Per drift seed d:
`T_null(d)` = max R over the 8. Commit `null_calibration.csv` before fitting
any held-out seed.

## Step 3 — held-out
Fit the 4 eligible transitions and 3 held-out no-forks.

## Gates (fixed now)
* **X1** every eligible transition: R > T_null (drift seed 0).
* **X2** every held-out no-fork: R ≤ T_null (drift seed 0).
* **X3** every eligible transition: |detected bin − true bin| ≤ 1 (drift seed 0).
* **X4** X1–X3 also hold for drift seeds 1 and 2 (each with its own T_null).
Report also fork AUC, P, and the S14 numbers for comparison.
