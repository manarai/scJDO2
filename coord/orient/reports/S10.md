## Deviations

* Executor: a local Claude agent. Commit footers are 'Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5' (the caller's instruction), not the Manus footer, so the checker warns on each commit.
* Start state: git status showed untracked S09 leftovers (v3_calibration/.locks, candidates/, s08_runtime/). They were left untouched and not committed.
* Step 0b pulse batches: implemented as a generator config field (pulse_batch_effect, True only for v3_transition). The factors come from their own seeded stream and are folded into batch_gene_effects; batch_day indexes the 6 (day, pulse) groups. The new fields are dropped from the stored metadata at their defaults, so v1/v2/v3 outputs stay byte-identical.
* z1_crit drives mu, the cycle-exit gate, the fate label and the fate genes' gate (z1_crit + 0.2). The on / on_off switching thresholds (theta) are not tied to Z1_CRIT and were left unchanged.
* Labelling smoothing follows labelling_diag diag.py: kNN-30 in the unit's embedding over all cells (both pulses), normalised by the total-count size factor, then multiplied back by the size factor before the S02 chain rule. Per-pulse gamma = one LabelledRateModel(seed=0) per pulse group.
* Gate variant: the gates say 'raw labelling' (J_flow = MeasuredDrift on the raw velocity, shared gamma), while Step 0b names smoothed + shared gamma the primary variant. Gates are evaluated on raw_shared; the primary variant is reported next to each gate.
* MeasuredDrift is fitted on its default device (MPS); the fitted network is moved to the CPU for jacobian_autodiff. Same function, about 10x faster.
* scVelo: the exact S03 block (s06_user_runs.run_scvelo, unedited). recover_dynamics and velocity_graph receive n_jobs=8 through thin wrappers (runtime only; gene fits are independent). One scVelo unit (v3_default s0) was killed after 7 min to apply this and then rerun. scVelo also ran on v3_stationary_cycle, for the Step 4 null.
* Palantir: s06_user_runs.run_palantir, unedited. S06's root rule was adapted to v3 days (5 day-2 cells rng(1000+seed), 5 day-6 cells rng(2000+seed)). Cycle null: one random cell rng(1000+seed), default mode.
* OT: POT ot.sinkhorn, balanced (moscot not installed; no installs). Growth weights: the generator divides every cell at the same rate, so the source masses are uniform within a day; total = exp(dt log2/16 h)(1 - f_sample) n_source/n_target. reg_m was read as c x eps, c in {0.1, 1, 10}.
* Step 2: forward_developmental_truth (protected) raises for v3, so no forward or span-matched truth exists. Development agreement uses the clone chord truth as a labelled substitute, and the gate clause is reported as not evaluable. Flow combinations were scored once per unit, not once per Palantir run, because they do not use pseudotime. The all-four independence check covers the gate and primary variants. Step 2 ran as one unit per preset after a single all-preset unit was stopped at 18 min (projected over 60 min).
* Step 4: Spearman 'before the true transition' = bin medians of bins 0..true-1 (and, separately, cells). The rising run = consecutive bin-to-bin increases ending at the detected bin. A strict-mode-root ordering was added as a report-only sensitivity. The J_flow_ot null is not computable on the one-time-point cycle.
* Step 6: horizons 6/24/48 h are nested time points of the same 32 paths per cell (one simulation); covariance ddof=1. Truth units were first 4 chunks per seed: 8 hit the 60-min limit under contention and were killed, and 4 more were killed for contention. Rerunning alone still projected about 45-60 min per chunk, so the truth was recomputed in 12 chunks per seed (seed = chunk index, 32-cell blocks; all 36 units 12.5-14 min). The 4-chunk outputs were set aside (superseded_4chunk/), not used.
* Step 6 corrections: clone dispersion is reported uncorrected, with Sigma_meas subtracted literally (task text; gate), and minus (Sigma_meas,t2 - Sigma_meas,t1)/(2 dt). OT: uncorrected and minus trace(Sigma_meas)/d (task text; gate). Sigma_meas = d d^T / 2 per cell from one redraw (noise seed 1).
* Step 6 OT eps selection (underspecified): executor's construction. For each eps in {0.25,0.5,1,2,4} x eps0: plan between the earlier day and 80 % of the later day; prediction = barycentric image + N(0, eps/2) per dim; criterion = Sinkhorn divergence (reg eps0, transport-cost form) to the 20 % held-out cells. The rule chose the boundary 0.25 x eps0 on every pair.
* Step 5: S11's arrays were absent, so they were rebuilt with S11's functions (imported; outputs redirected to data/s10/real); the LARRY OT velocity reproduces S11 exactly. FA-30 was added as the second embedding, and fit seeds 0-2 as 'seeds of the fit'. Palantir roots: LARRY day-2 cell rng(1000); scNT-seq HSC cell rng(1000) (the dataset has no days). The scNT labelling gamma range (0.0025-5.65) differs slightly from S11's (0.0027-5.64), from MPS non-determinism.

* S08 runtime copy moved into the gitignored data folder (data/s10/s08_runtime); the v3_transition rows are committed as s08_v3_transition_stats.csv.

## Observations

* J_flow_ot (time course alone, PCA) separates saddle from committed cells with AUC 0.97–1.00 on v3_default/stress/batch and 0.82–0.97 on v3_transition; its leading eigenvector at saddle cells aligns with the fate axis (|cos| 0.97–0.99, v3_default). In FA its AUC is 0.04–0.25 (inverted) on v3_default/stress/batch.
* Labelling J_flow AUC (PCA): raw variants 0.50–0.85, kNN-smoothed variants 0.70–0.93 on v3_default/stress/batch and 0.52–0.61 on v3_transition; per-pulse γ changes the AUC by at most 0.05 relative to shared γ (v3_transition, which has pulse batches, included).
* kNN-smoothed labelling velocity orients worse than raw ({labelling} agreement 0.62-0.67 vs 0.95-0.98).
* v3_default has only 22-29 saddle cells per seed (fork crossed at ~30 h, before the first sample); v3_transition has 350-368.
* scVelo-based J_flow AUC 0.27-0.57 and scVelo orientation 0.46-0.57 (splicing; v3 caveats).
* On v3_transition seed 1 (the only informative seed), J_flow and J_flow_ot lambda1 peak exactly at the true bin (5), and J_flow rises over 4 bins before it.
* Measurement-noise displacement (median trace(Sigma_meas)/d 0.15-0.17 in PCA) exceeds the 48-h effective diffusion (0.088-0.105).
* Clone dispersion has 45-55 usable clone pairs per seed, all day 4 -> 6.
* On scNT-seq the raw and smoothed labelling J_flow locate the lambda1 peak at opposite ends of the HSC-rooted ordering (bin 0, HSC/MEP vs bin 10, Meg/Ery), stable across embeddings and fit seeds.
* LARRY: Palantir default mode moved the day-2 root to a day-6 neutrophil; J_flow_ot lambda1 peaks in bin 7 (96 % Undifferentiated) for every PCA fit seed.
* Moving the fork (z1_crit 2.0) leaves the S08 realism count unchanged (6/13 per seed); participation ratio rises 8.0-8.9 -> 9.1-9.3.

## Suggestions

* Redesign the transition test so the fork falls mid-ordering: day-balanced resampling (e.g. equal cells per day) or an earlier sampling day, then rerun Step 4 only (the operators are fitted already).
* Use Palantir strict mode for the guard and the binning, or report both: default mode snaps day-2 roots to late cells on v3.
* Add a multi-time stationary control (the stationary cycle sampled at 2-3 days) so the OT operator has a null.
* Specify the OT diffusion selection rule and the Sigma_meas correction; the truth arrays (data/s10/v3_default_s*/diffusion) can be reused without recomputation.
* Investigate why OT-flow operators invert in FA (AUC <= 0.25) while PCA gives >= 0.97: possibly the FA posterior map's scaling of the OT cost.

## Questions

1. Critical-transition gate is untestable as designed. With 12 equal-count bins and cells by day [187, 1448, 11066], mu crosses 0 inside bin 0-1: true bin 1 / 5 / 0, and seed 1's bin 5 comes from a default-mode Palantir start on day 6. Which redesign applies: day-balanced subsampling before binning, bins on another quantity, more early cells (sampling), or another validity rule?
2. Orientation gate: authorise a v3 forward developmental truth (a new generator function; the protected one raises for v3)? Or should the {lineage, pseudotime} clause use the chord truth?
3. J_flow_ot transition gate: the null cannot be computed on v3_stationary_cycle, which has one sampling time. Use the J_flow null, a multi-time stationary control, or drop the clause?
4. Step 6 OT: what exactly should the held-out Sinkhorn divergence compare? The executor's construction always chose the smallest eps (grid boundary).
5. Step 6 Sigma_meas: the literal correction (Cov difference/(2 dt) minus Sigma_meas; D_OT minus trace(Sigma_meas)/d) subtracts a quantity larger than the signal and makes every estimate negative. Was the differenced form intended?
6. Guard gate: Palantir default mode moves the start off day 2 in 30/30 v3_default runs, so the §16 rule refuses them all (strict mode accepts 30/30). Is the gate meant for strict mode only, or does the default-mode failure stand?
