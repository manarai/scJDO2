# orient/S13 — done

## Summary
S13 completed the preregistered whitening comparison on six full-size v3 synthetic datasets (PCA-10/20/50 and FA-20) and fixed-sample LARRY (PCA-30 and FA-30).
The fixed gate **failed**: 0/4 linear embeddings had whitened-flow λ₁ fork AUC ≥0.85 on every `v3_transition` seed; this is reported without any retuning.
Whitened-flow median AUC was lower than raw S12 flow for all three PCA embeddings; FA-20 remained low under both paths.
First-pair-only LARRY PCA-30 whitening improved on no movement (energy 0.751 vs 0.854), whereas the three synthetic transition flow forecasts did not beat no movement.
All 26 dataset × embedding units were detached, timed, checkpointed, and under the 60-minute/24-GB stops. Timed CPU was 0.702 h / 8 h, and the final required suite passed 48/48.

4. Ran detached aggregation to write AUC, agreement, partial-variant, held-out-prediction tables and figures. The initial presentational formatter failure was repaired without touching analysis data or parameters, then aggregation was retried once.
5. Ran the detached CPU ledger and validated both PNGs as nonblank, decodable images.
6. Ran `python -m pytest -q tests/test_orient.py tests/test_smoke.py`: **48 passed** in 6.17 s pytest elapsed.

## Observations
- The full whitening path yielded lower transition median AUC than raw flow for PCA-10 (0.320 vs 0.895), PCA-20 (0.426 vs 0.910), and PCA-50 (0.389 vs 0.891); FA-20 was 0.490 whitened versus 0.475 raw.
- Median λ₁ agreement was low across all embedding pairs (0.068–0.282), despite the linear relationship among the PCA embeddings.
- LARRY PCA-30 was the only flow forecast among the stated examples that improved upon no movement; LARRY FA-30’s mean-displacement baseline was best overall.
- All timed analysis logs stayed within resource limits; maximum recorded RSS was 4.653 GB and the ledger was 0.699 CPU-hours before final tests.

## Suggestions
- If approved as a new task, separate the effect of whitening on the OT coupling from its effect on MeasuredDrift regularisation using a newly frozen diagnostic.
- If predictive transport becomes a selection criterion, preregister its baseline comparison and decision threshold before comparing alternate embeddings or estimators.
