## Observations

* PCA-20 is itself below 0.85 on v3_transition s0 (0.818).
* The AUC is not monotone in PCA dimension: PCA-10 is the best, and PCA-50 drops to 0.56–0.64 on v3_default s0 and s2.
* scVI-10 is weak on v3_transition (0.31–0.56), and its leading eigenvector at saddle cells is poorly aligned with the fate axis (|cos| 0.17–0.53).
* In FA-20, committed cells sit about 1 unit from the saddle midpoint along the fate axis (PCA-20: 7–11 units). The median OT speed is 0.05–0.10 (PCA-20: 0.17–0.28).
* On v3_transition, the MeasuredDrift-fitted velocities agree between FA and PCA much less than the raw OT velocities do: median cos 0.09–0.28 vs 0.54–0.56.

## Suggestions

* Sweep the PCA dimension (5–50) on the development data, to find where OT flow J stops resolving the fork.
* Rescale the FA axes, e.g. by posterior precision, before OT and MeasuredDrift. This would separate an OT-cost effect from a MeasuredDrift scale/regularisation effect.
* Prefer v3_transition (350–368 saddle cells) over v3_default (22–29) as the fork set: the v3_default AUCs are noisy.
