## Revision 1

The initial report correctly identified a root-rule contradiction and a review then identified an undeclared S3 deviation. Both have been resolved without refitting unaffected readouts.

Each of the 16 splicing-capable datasets has exactly these four classes and shares summing to 1. The previous scVelo likelihood/switch-time categories are retained only as the explicitly named auxiliary statistic `S3b_scvelo_switch_class`.
2. **T2:** `dentate` uses literal `obs["age(days)"]`; its recorded earliest root age is 12. `pancreas` has no time field, so it is recorded as `T2_not_applicable` and has no substituted annotation or stale Palantir-entropy result.
