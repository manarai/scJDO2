# orient/S19 — dyngen datasets and realism

Status: complete.

Generated nine dyngen datasets: bifurcating, cycle, and linear backbones, each with seeds 0–2. Every dataset has 5,000 cells, 1,000 features, 32 simulations, synchronised sampling at three time points, raw spliced/unspliced layers, prefixed truth fields, truth RNA velocity, GRN, and milestone-network metadata.
